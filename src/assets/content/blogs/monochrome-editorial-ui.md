When looking back at centuries of print editorial design, books, and typography, every hairline rule and point size served a deliberate communicative purpose. Nothing was decorative noise.

In mobile engineering, we often fall into the trap of over-decorating screens with bright multi-colored buttons and pulsing animations. By restricting our palette to deep black ink and paper ivory, visual hierarchy relies purely on font weight, scale, and whitespace.

When you introduce color in such an environment, even a small 6-pixel crimson dot commands 100% of the user’s cognitive attention. It becomes a true priority signal rather than wallpaper.
